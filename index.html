Hi, I'm Deep — a postgrad chemical engineering student at IIT Madras, hooked on building at the intersection of science and intelligent systems.

My work focuses on Scientific Machine Learning — from predicting adsorption energies of catalysts using ML models to building LLM-powered agentic pipelines that mine literature at scale for data curation. I'm particularly excited about how local LLM systems and automated scientific workflows can accelerate research that would otherwise take years.

Outside of research, I have a background in process engineering (Zentiva, TITLIS), graphic design leadership, and a published patent. I'm driven by curiosity, collaboration, and the belief that sustainable energy solutions live at the intersection of data and domain expertise.
